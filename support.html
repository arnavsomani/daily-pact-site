Common questions

How is today’s number calculated?

Daily Pact takes your monthly budget, sets aside your fixed costs, and spreads what’s left across the days remaining in the month.

Example: a $6,000 budget with $3,000 of fixed costs over a 30-day month leaves $3,000 for daily spending, which is $100 a day.

Today’s number stays the same all day. What you spend today comes out of it, and the new number for tomorrow is worked out at midnight.

What happens when I overspend, or spend less?

The difference spreads over the rest of the month automatically. In the example above, spending $160 on day 1 leaves $2,840 for 29 days, so each remaining day becomes $97.93. Spending only $40 leaves $2,960, so each day becomes $102.06.

If the money for daily spending runs out, today’s number shows 0 and the app tells you plainly how far over you are. It never shows a negative number. A new month starts fresh: leftovers and overspending don’t carry over.

How do fixed costs work?

Fixed costs are things you pay every month, such as rent, a cleaner or a phone plan. Their planned amounts are set aside before your daily number is worked out. Each month starts with every fixed cost Unpaid. You can:

  • Mark paid: one tap pays the planned amount today. You can also enter a different amount or date.
  • Add part payment: record one installment at a time, for example “$1,500 of $2,500 paid”.
  • Skip this month: the planned amount goes back into daily spending for this month only.

Turn on “Remind me on the due day” in a fixed cost to get a reminder at 9 am on its due date.

What if a fixed cost costs more than planned?

Enter the amount you actually paid. The extra is taken from daily spending and shared across the remaining days. For example, if the cleaner’s planned $400 is paid as $450, the extra $50 lowers each remaining day a little. If it costs less, the difference goes back into daily spending.

Does every cent really add up?

Yes. Each day’s number is rounded down to the cent (or your currency’s smallest unit), so the app never promises money that isn’t there, and the last day of the month gets exactly what’s left. “Where the money went”, on the home screen, shows a breakdown that always balances exactly.

How do I invite my partner, or more people?

Sharing is part of Daily Pact Pro. Only the person who bought Pro needs it: everyone they invite shares the budget for free.

  1. Open Settings → Sharing → Invite partner.
  2. Choose how to send the invitation and pick the person by their Apple ID email or phone number.
  3. They open the link on their iPhone, with Daily Pact installed, and tap Join.

A budget can be shared with up to 6 people, including you: a partner, family or roommates. Everyone needs to be signed in to iCloud. If someone already has their own budget, it’s saved to History when they join, and they can restore it if they leave.

What can the owner do that others can’t?

The owner is the person who created the budget and sent the invitations. Only the owner can change the budget amount, currency, time zone and start day; add, change or remove fixed costs; add money or set money aside; and invite or remove people.

Everyone can add, edit and delete purchases, and mark fixed costs paid or skipped, because anyone may be the one who pays the rent.

What happens when someone leaves?

Anyone can leave in Settings → Sharing → Leave this budget. What they added stays in the budget under their name, so the totals still add up. Their iPhone keeps a read-only copy of the history so far, in History.

The same happens if the owner removes someone, or uses Stop sharing for everyone.

Why can’t I link my bank account?

On purpose. Daily Pact works with the numbers you type in, so it never needs your bank login and never sees your accounts. That keeps your finances private and means there’s nothing to break when a bank changes its systems. Logging a purchase takes two taps: the amount, then Save.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the Daily Pact Pro screen. Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you used to buy. Pro is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing. If Restore doesn’t find it, email us with the date of purchase. Don’t send your password.

How do I export or delete everything?

Export: Settings → Export this month (CSV) (Pro) creates a spreadsheet file of your spending that opens in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.

Delete: Settings → Delete all data deletes everything on your iPhone and in your iCloud. If you own a shared budget, this also ends sharing for everyone, so the app warns you and offers to export first. If you joined someone else’s budget, you leave it. After deleting, a reinstall starts empty.

Deleting only the app does not delete your iCloud copy. If you own a shared budget, it keeps working for the others until you reinstall and delete it, or stop sharing.

My partner and I see different numbers.

Both iPhones need to be online and signed in to iCloud. Changes usually arrive within a few seconds. Open the app on both phones and wait a moment. If it still differs, check that iCloud is allowed for Daily Pact on both phones: iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Saved to iCloud → See All, then email us.
