Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

In short: we don’t collect any of your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your budget is stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, and it is shared only with people you invite.

This policy covers the Daily Pact Budget app for iPhone (“Daily Pact”, “the app”) and this website. Daily Pact is made by Arnav Somani, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). If you have a question, email arnavsomani1992@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. We don’t run a server, and the app never sends your information to us or to anyone else we work with. We have no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We can’t see your budget, your purchases, your name or your Apple ID.

What the app stores, and where

To work, the app keeps the information you enter:

  • your budget, currency and cycle start day;
  • your fixed costs (for example rent) and whether they’re paid;
  • the purchases you log, with any notes;
  • the name you type on the first screen, shown next to what you add;
  • settings on this iPhone, such as reminders and theme.

This information is kept in two places, both under your control:

  1. On your iPhone. It includes a small summary for the Home Screen and Lock Screen widget.
  2. In your own iCloud account, using Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit) private database, so it syncs to your other devices and comes back if you reinstall. This is your storage with Apple, not ours, and Apple’s privacy policy applies to it. If you aren’t signed in to iCloud, the data stays only on your iPhone.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server.

Sharing a budget

If you buy Daily Pact Pro, you can share a budget with up to five other people. Sharing works only through Apple’s iCloud sharing:

  • You choose each person by their Apple ID email address or phone number. An invitation link works only for the people you invite; it can’t be opened by anyone else.
  • Everyone in a shared budget can see its budget, fixed costs, purchases and notes, and the names of the other people in it.
  • The owner can remove people or stop sharing at any time, and anyone can leave. When someone’s access ends, their iPhone keeps a read-only copy of the history up to that point, stored only on that iPhone.

We don’t take part in sharing and can’t see shared budgets.

Purchases

Daily Pact Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Apple tells us only aggregate sales figures, which don’t identify you.

No bank connections

Daily Pact never connects to your bank, cards, SMS messages or any financial account. It handles no real money. Every number in it is one you typed in.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in iPhone Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don’t include your budget data. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Delete all data. This deletes everything on your iPhone and in your iCloud:

  • If you own a budget, its data is deleted from your iCloud, which also ends sharing for everyone in it. The app warns you first and offers to export a copy.
  • If you joined someone else’s budget, you leave it, and your local copy and any read-only history are deleted. What you added stays in the owner’s budget, under your name, because it is part of their record.

After deleting, a reinstall starts empty. Deleting only the app removes the data on that iPhone, but your iCloud copy remains until you use Delete all data or remove it in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. You can export your spending to a CSV file at any time from Settings (Pro).

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 give you rights over your personal data: to know what is held, to get a copy, to correct it and to delete it.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to give, correct or delete. You can see, change, export and delete everything yourself in the app, as described above. We don’t sell or share personal information, and we don’t use it for targeted advertising or profiling.

If you have a question or complaint about privacy, email arnavsomani1992@gmail.com. We’ll reply within 30 days. You can also contact your local data protection authority.

Children

Daily Pact is not directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a change ever means we would collect data, we’ll say so in the app before it happens.
